Benchmark terminology

Recall
Detected source-positive emails divided by all source-positive emails. Report broad spam/fraud positives separately from publisher-phishing positives.
False-positive rate (FPR)
Legitimate-labeled emails alerted divided by all legitimate-labeled emails. Zero alerts on 41 controls does not establish zero real-world false alarms.
Balanced accuracy
Mean of recall and specificity (1 minus FPR). Gives the two classes equal weight despite 249:41 imbalance.
MCC
Matthews correlation coefficient: a summary of all four confusion counts, ranging from -1 to 1. Undefined if its denominator is zero.
ECE
Expected calibration error: the weighted gap between mean scores and observed positive rates in ten fixed bins. It depends on this cohort and its prevalence.
Calibration
A validation-only mapping from model probabilities to research risk scores. It does not turn a balanced dataset into representative inbox probabilities.
2% validation target
Each model cutoff is selected on its separate legitimate validation cohort. It is not a promise of 2% false alarms on every future source.
p95 latency
95% of the 100 measured responses are at or below this duration. Performance.now is used inside Chrome; model-only and full local workflow times are kept separately.
Cold readiness
Fresh-context elapsed navigation time through loading plus the first usable classification and software explanation. Local assets and OS cache affect it.
Quantization
Compact integer weight representation. It can change scores, and different execution backends need not produce identical quantized probabilities.
Group-bootstrap CI
95% uncertainty interval obtained by resampling dependent text families within class strata, seed 42. Its quality depends on grouping and the source sample.
Diagnostic leader
Highest observed operational balanced-accuracy point estimate, with false alarms and speed available as tie considerations. It is not an original all-gates qualified winner.
McNemar test
Paired comparison of which emails each model gets right. Holm correction accounts for the reported multiple comparisons. Template dependence limits its assumptions.
Zero generated tokens
These are classifiers, not text generators. They still tokenize and compute on input text; explanations are produced by a separate deterministic English software layer.
